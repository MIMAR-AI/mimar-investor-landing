THE THESIS

AI agents complete tasks.
MIMAR turns them into controlled, reusable workflows.

AI agents are getting easier to access. The harder problem is turning a series of tasks into dependable work that moves across agents, tools and people. Teams still need shared context, checks they can trust, and clear moments for human decisions.

THE CURRENT STATE

Work fragments

Prompts, files, outputs and decisions end up across several tools. Each handoff costs context, so the team has to reconstruct what happened before it can move on.

THE WEDGE

Start where lost context is already expensive.

MIMAR starts with recurring product release work because a missed detail or broken handoff already has a visible cost there. These workflows cross agents, tools, checks, evidence and human decisions.

CHAT TOOLSGood for individual tasks. When work returns later or spans several stages, the team often has to rebuild the context.

AGENT TOOLSGood at executing a run. Context, approvals and ownership can still break across runs and handoffs.

MIMARKeeps the context, work, artifacts, evidence, approvals and history connected from one run to the next.

EXPANSIONProduct release workflows, then product operations, then wider delivery workflows

HOW TO READ THE FORECAST

One operating plan, built one cohort at a time.

Commercial Year 1 starts with the paid Beta customers and adds new acquisition. Year 2 retains part of both groups and adds a new cohort. Year 3 does the same across every earlier cohort. The model assumes 90% annual B2B customer retention, 60% individual retention and 70% retention for active API usage accounts.

CORE BETA SUCCESS CRITERIA
  • 30 paid Beta individuals at exit
  • 2 founding Team Pro workspaces with at least 6 seats in total
  • $7.14K in Beta bookings before commercial launch
  • Test the 90% B2B renewal target after 12 months
ENGAGE VALIDATION MILESTONESPaid workspaces, completed implementations, usage backed by evidence, retention and account expansion.
Research behind the model +

We checked the pricing against current official plans from Manychat, respond.io, Gorgias, OpenAI Business and Claude Team. Retention assumptions use ChartMogul. Conversion and growth targets use its conversion report and go to market report. Margin and customer acquisition cost benchmarks come from Benchmarkit and Bessemer.

The Beta figures are targets we are working to close, not reported results. The $7.14K booking target combines 30 Individual Pro founding passes at $190 a year, worth $5.7K, with two Founding Team Pro workspaces. Each team starts with three seats at $240 per seat a year, worth $1.44K in total. Annual bookings are recognized as revenue over the contract period. The 90% B2B retention figure remains a management target until renewal data is available. The model assumes a 30% gross margin on onboarding. Prepaid API credits count as revenue only when customers use them. Enterprise revenue is excluded from Commercial Year 1 until a contract is signed. Marketing spend rises in stages and only after conversion, retention and customer acquisition cost remain within plan.
