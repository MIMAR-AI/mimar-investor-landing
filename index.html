HOW TO READ THE FORECAST

One operating plan, built cohort by cohort.

Commercial Year 1 starts with the paid Beta cohort plus new acquisition. Year 2 retains both prior groups and adds its new cohort. Year 3 compounds every prior cohort. The plan uses 90% annual B2B logo retention, 60% individual retention, and 70% active API-wallet retention.

WHAT VALIDATES THE MODEL
  • 30 paid Beta individuals at exit
  • 5 paid Beta workspaces · 15 minimum seats
  • $9.3K Beta bookings before commercial GA
  • 90% B2B renewal target tested at 12 months
Research basis and model boundaries +

Prices were compared with current official pricing from Manychat, respond.io, Gorgias, OpenAI Business, and Claude Team. Retention uses ChartMogul; conversion and growth targets use its conversion and go-to-market benchmarks. Margin and customer-acquisition-cost context use Benchmarkit and Bessemer.

Beta targets are management targets being closed now, not actual results. The $240 Beta team price is a planning assumption pending signed contracts. Annual bookings are recognized as revenue ratably. B2B retention is a disclosed management target pending renewal evidence. Onboarding is modeled separately at 30% gross margin. Unused prepaid API credits are not recognized until consumed. Enterprise revenue is excluded from Commercial Year 1 until contracted. Marketing spending is released in stages and increases only when conversion, retention, and customer-acquisition cost remain within the plan.
